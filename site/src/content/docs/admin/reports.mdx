When someone on Nostr reports a post, a profile or a file (a NIP-56 report), it lands in **Reports**. The red number next to Reports in the sidebar is how many are waiting.

## Read a report

Each card tells you:

- **the type**, such as spam, impersonation, nudity, profanity, illegal, malware or other;
- **who reported whom**, with both people's names; click one to open their page;
- **the reporter's comment**, in quotes;
- **the reported content**: the note itself, or the reported file. Reported images are blurred and never load on their own; hover or tap to look;
- **"N reports on target"** when several reports name the same thing.

Sometimes a report names one person but points at content someone else posted. The card then warns that the report names someone *instead of the content's author*, and any ban goes to the author.

Use the tabs to switch between **Open**, **Resolved** and **Dismissed** reports, and the menu on the right to show one type only.

## Act on it

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Dismiss** | Nothing is removed; the report moves to Dismissed. |
| **Delete** | The reported event is deleted. |
| **Delete & ban** | The event is deleted and its author banned. |
| **Ban user** | The author is banned; shown when the report is about a person rather than a post. |
| **⋯ → Ban user only** | Ban without deleting the post. |
| **⋯ → Delete media** | Delete the reported file. |

Everything except Dismiss asks you to confirm and lets you add a **resolution note**. Resolved reports show what was done, by whom, when, and your note.

To handle several at once, tick their boxes (or **Select all**) and use **Dismiss**, **Delete** or **Delete & ban** in the bar at the bottom.

## On your phone

Swipe a report right to dismiss it, or left to ban (and delete the reported event, if there is one).

<Image src={phone} alt="The Reports page on a phone, with a hint 'Swipe right to dismiss, left to ban' above a report card." width={390} />

<Aside title="Never miss one">
Turn on [push notifications](/relay/admin/notifications/) for **New reports** and your phone tells you when a report comes in; tapping it opens that report. If people keep reporting the same account, **Auto-ban after N reports** in [Settings → Moderation](/relay/admin/settings/#moderation) can ban it for you. A word filter set to **Report** also files matching events here.
</Aside>

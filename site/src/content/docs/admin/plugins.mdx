Plugins add features to your relay: extra policies, integrations, admin pages and dashboard cards. **Plugins** lists every plugin the relay has loaded.

<Image src={plugins} alt="The Plugins page with two plugin cards, 'hello' and 'spam-filter', each with a version, a green 'running' badge, a description, an on/off switch and a Settings link; the hello card also links to its Hello page." />

Each card shows the plugin's name, version and status: **running**, **disabled**, **error**, or **UI failed** when its admin page couldn't load. If something went wrong, the error is shown in red on the card.

## Turn a plugin on or off

Use the switch on the card. The change takes effect right away, without restarting the relay.

## Change its settings

Click **Settings** (or the plugin's name) to open its page. Besides the on/off switch, it shows a **Settings** form generated from what the plugin accepts, with a short explanation for each field. Changes are applied to the running plugin when you save. A plugin without options says *This plugin has no configurable settings.*

## Plugin pages and cards

A plugin can bring its own admin pages. They show up in a **Plugins** group in the sidebar (under **More** on a phone), as links on the plugin's card, and in the [command palette](/relay/admin/#search-and-jump-anywhere). Plugins can also add cards to the bottom of the [dashboard](/relay/admin/dashboard/) and actions to the **⋯** menus on [users](/relay/admin/users/).

## Installing plugins

The admin app doesn't install plugins. You add them in `beamhop.config.json` (npm packages or local folders) and restart the relay; after that you manage them here. If none are installed, the page says so. To write your own, see [Plugins & API](/relay/build/).

<Aside title="Permissions">
Everyone except viewers can open plugin pages (`plugins.use`); every role can see the list (`plugins.view`); turning plugins on and off and changing their settings needs `plugins.manage` (owners and admins).
</Aside>

The **Admins** page lists everyone who can sign in to the admin app, with their role. Only owners see it.

<Image src={admins} alt="The Admins page: a list of people with a role picker next to each (owner, moderator, viewer), and a Role permissions card describing each role with its permission badges." />

## The four roles

| Role | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including managing other admins. |
| **Admin** | Everything except managing admins. |
| **Moderator** | Users, content, reports and media moderation. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access. |

The **Role permissions** card at the bottom of the page lists every permission each role has. In detail:

The app hides what a role can't use, and the server refuses it anyway.

## Add an admin

<Steps>

1. On **Admins**, click **Add admin**.
2. Paste their npub or hex pubkey and pick a role.
3. Click **Grant access**. They can now [sign in](/relay/admin/signing-in/) with their Nostr key.

</Steps>

You can also set a role from anyone's [user page](/relay/admin/users/#a-users-page), in the **Admin role** card.

## Change or remove a role

Pick a new role from the menu next to the person. **Remove access** takes their role away after you confirm. On a user's page, making someone an owner asks for confirmation too, since owners can manage every other admin.

You can't change your own role, so there is always someone who can undo a mistake.

<Aside title="From the command line">
The server operator can manage admins without the app: `beamhop admin add npub1… --role moderator`, and `beamhop admin remove npub1…`, which also ends their sessions, push notifications, password, TOTP and passkeys. Owners listed in `BEAMHOP_OWNERS` always keep the owner role.
</Aside>

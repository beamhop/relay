Every beamhop relay comes with an admin app. It runs in your browser at `/admin/` on your relay, for example `https://relay.example.com/admin/`, and it is where you watch traffic, look after people and content, and change how the relay behaves. There is nothing extra to install or host: the same process that serves the relay serves the app.

<Image src={dashboard} alt="The admin app's dashboard: a sidebar with Overview, Community, Content and System sections, and cards showing connections, events per second, storage, CPU and memory, above traffic and system charts." />

The screenshots in this guide come from a demo relay called Driftwood, filled with invented people and posts.

## Finding your way around

On a computer, the sidebar on the left groups the pages:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | [Dashboard](/relay/admin/dashboard/), [Live](/relay/admin/live/) |
| **Community** | [Users](/relay/admin/users/), [Admins](/relay/admin/roles/), [Invites](/relay/admin/invites/), [IP rules](/relay/admin/ip-rules/) |
| **Content** | [Events](/relay/admin/events/), [Reports](/relay/admin/reports/), [Media](/relay/admin/media/), [Groups](/relay/admin/groups/) |
| **System** | [Settings](/relay/admin/settings/), [Plugins](/relay/admin/plugins/), [Audit log](/relay/admin/audit-log/), [System](/relay/admin/system/), [Account](/relay/admin/account/) |

Plugins can add their own pages; they appear in an extra **Plugins** group. You only see the pages your [role](/relay/admin/roles/) allows, so a viewer's sidebar is shorter than an owner's. **System** and **Account** are always there.

A few things stay on screen wherever you are:

- **The live dot** in the top bar. Green means the app is receiving live updates from the relay; amber means it is reconnecting; red means it is offline.
- **The red number next to Reports** counts open reports waiting for you.
- **Your name** at the bottom of the sidebar opens a menu with Account & security, the theme (light, dark or system) and Sign out.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> on Windows and Linux) collapses the sidebar to icons.

### On a phone

On a narrow screen the sidebar becomes a bar at the bottom with **Dashboard**, **Users**, **Events** and **Reports**. Everything else is under **More**, along with the theme and Sign out. Lists work with your thumb: swipe a row to act on it and long-press to start selecting several. Each page explains its gestures.

<Image src={more} alt="The admin app on a phone, with the More sheet open showing tiles for Live, Admins, Invites, IP rules, Media, Groups, Settings, Plugins, Audit log, System, Account and a plugin page called Hello." width={390} />

## Search and jump anywhere

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), or click **Search…** in the top bar, to open the command palette. Type part of a page name to jump to it. You can also paste:

- an `npub` or hex pubkey, to open that user or list their events;
- a `note1…` id or hex event id, to find that event;
- any other text, to search users or run a full-text search over events.

The palette also switches the theme and signs you out.

<Image src={palette} alt="The command palette open over the dashboard, with the text 'jun' typed and options to search users and events for it." />

## Install it as an app

The admin app is a Progressive Web App: you can install it so it opens in its own window, with its own icon, like any other app. It keeps working on a flaky connection (a simple offline page shows when the relay can't be reached), and installing it is what makes [push notifications](/relay/admin/notifications/) possible on iPhone and iPad.

<Tabs syncKey="pwa">
<TabItem label="Chrome / Edge">

<Steps>

1. Open your relay's `/admin/` page and sign in.
2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose **Install beamhop admin** (in Edge: **Apps → Install this site as an app**).
3. Confirm. The app opens in its own window and is added to your apps.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone / iPad">

<Steps>

1. Open your relay's `/admin/` page in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**. Open beamhop from your home screen from now on.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">

<Steps>

1. Open your relay's `/admin/` page in Chrome.
2. Open the menu (⋮) and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm. beamhop appears with your other apps.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

When a new version of beamhop is deployed, the app picks it up on its own and shows a **New version available** message with a **Reload** button.

<Aside title="Use HTTPS">
Browsers only install apps, run the offline support and allow push notifications on secure pages: `https://…`, or `http://localhost` while you try things out. Put your relay behind a [reverse proxy with TLS](/relay/deploy/reverse-proxy/) before you rely on the app day to day.
</Aside>

## Who can use it

Only people with an admin role can sign in. The first owner comes from your configuration (`BEAMHOP_OWNERS` or `beamhop admin add npub1… --role owner`); after that, owners can give others access from the [Admins](/relay/admin/roles/) page. See [Signing in](/relay/admin/signing-in/) for the ways to log in.

The admin app can send push notifications to your devices, so you hear about new reports and problems without keeping it open.

<Image src={prefs} alt="The lower part of the Account page: the Notifications card with 'New reports' and 'System alerts' ticked and an 'Enable notifications' button, next to the Privacy card with two switches, and the Appearance card below." />

## Turn them on

<Steps>

1. On the device you want notified, open [Account](/relay/admin/account/) and scroll to **Notifications**.
2. Tick the topics you want:
   - **New reports**: when a user files a report.
   - **System alerts**: rejection spikes, memory and event-loop pressure, auto-bans and word-filter matches.
3. Click **Enable notifications** and allow notifications when the browser asks.

</Steps>

That's it for this device. Repeat on each phone or computer you want notified. Change the topics any time by ticking or unticking them; **Disable on this device** turns notifications off here.

Tapping a notification opens the matching page: a new report opens that report in the [Reports](/relay/admin/reports/) queue, an alert opens the dashboard. When lots happen at once (a spam wave, say), you get the first right away and then one "N more" summary instead of a flood.

You only get topics your role can see: report notifications need access to reports, and alerts need the dashboard.

## If it says it can't

- *This browser doesn't support web push. On iOS, add the app to your home screen first.* On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work from the [installed app](/relay/admin/#install-it-as-an-app); open beamhop from your home screen and try again. Other browsers need a secure page (`https://`).
- *Push notifications are not configured on this server.* The server couldn't set up its push keys; check its logs.

<Aside title="Signing out stops them">
Signing out, ending a session on the [Account](/relay/admin/account/#sessions) page, or losing your admin role stops that device's notifications too, so a shared or lost device stops receiving alerts.
</Aside>

<Aside title="For operators">
Push works out of the box: beamhop creates its Web Push (VAPID) keys on first start and keeps them in the database. To use your own keys, set `BEAMHOP_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `BEAMHOP_VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` and `BEAMHOP_VAPID_SUBJECT`; see the [configuration reference](/relay/config/reference/#adminvapid).
</Aside>

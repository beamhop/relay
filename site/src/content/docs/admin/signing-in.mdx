Open `/admin/` on your relay. If you aren't signed in yet, you land on the sign-in screen. Pick whichever way suits you; they all end in the same place.

<Image src={login} alt="The sign-in screen: a 'Sign in with extension' button, then tabs for Signer, Key, Password and Passkey." />

Only people with an admin role (owner, admin, moderator or viewer) can get in. Anyone else is turned away with "not an admin", even with a valid key. To give someone access, see [Admins and roles](/relay/admin/roles/).

## The ways to sign in

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Extension">

The big **Sign in with extension** button uses a NIP-07 browser extension that holds your Nostr key. Click it and approve the request in the extension. Your key never leaves the extension.

If the page says *No NIP-07 extension detected*, the browser doesn't have one; use one of the tabs below instead.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Signer">

The **Signer** tab works with a remote signer (NIP-46), such as a signer app on your phone. You have two options:

- **Paste a `bunker://…` URL** (or a `name@domain` address that points to one) from your signer and click **Connect**.
- **Use `nostrconnect://` instead**: the app shows a connection link. Paste it into your signer, or tap **Open signer app** on the same device, then approve the request there. The screen waits for your signer until you do, or until you click **Cancel**.

If your signer wants you to confirm on a web page first, a link to that authorization page appears.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Key">

The **Key** tab takes your private key (`nsec1…` or 64-character hex). The browser signs the login itself; the key is not sent to the relay, not saved, and cleared from memory after signing in. The field is masked and tells password managers not to store it.

Use this when nothing else is at hand. An extension or remote signer is safer, because the key never has to be typed into a web page.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Password">

The **Password** tab is for signing in without any Nostr signer. Enter your npub, hex pubkey or your relay [NIP-05 name](/relay/admin/users/#details), and your password.

If you turned on two-factor authentication, an **Authenticator code** field appears after the first try: enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and sign in again.

There is no password until you set one. Set it on your [Account](/relay/admin/account/#password) page after signing in another way, or ask the server operator to run `beamhop admin password npub1…`.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Passkey">

The **Passkey** tab signs you in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or a security key, once you have [added a passkey](/relay/admin/account/#passkeys). Leave **Account** empty to let the browser offer your saved passkeys, or type your npub or name to narrow it down. Then click **Sign in with passkey**.

If the browser can't do passkeys, the tab says so.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## After you sign in

You land on the page you were trying to open, or the dashboard. Your session lasts a week from the last time you used it, and at most 30 days; after that, sign in again. You can see and end sessions on the [Account](/relay/admin/account/#sessions) page, and **Sign out** is in the menu under your name.

<Aside title="Locked out?">
After too many wrong attempts the sign-in screen answers *too many login attempts, try again later*; wait a little and try again. If you lost your authenticator or a passkey, the server operator can run `beamhop admin password npub1… --reset-2fa` to set a new password and remove TOTP and passkeys for that admin.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Passkeys and the public URL">
Passkeys are tied to the host name in your relay's public URL (`BEAMHOP_PUBLIC_URL`) and need a secure page (`https://`, or `localhost` while testing). Signing in through a different address, such as a LAN IP, won't offer them. Set the public URL to the address people really use; see the [configuration reference](/relay/config/reference/#server).
</Aside>

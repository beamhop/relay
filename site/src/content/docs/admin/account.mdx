**Account** is about you, not the relay: how you sign in, where you are signed in, and your preferences on this device. Open it from the sidebar or from the menu under your name (**Account & security**).

<Image src={account} alt="The Account page: a card with the admin's avatar, name, owner badge, npub and '25 permissions', a Sign out button, then cards for Password, Two-factor authentication, Passkeys and Sessions, where one session is marked 'this device'." />

The top card shows your name, role, npub (with a copy button) and how many permissions your role gives you.

## Password

A password lets you sign in without a Nostr signer, for example on a borrowed laptop. Enter a new password (at least 8 characters) twice and click **Set password**. To change it later you also enter the current one, unless you signed in with your Nostr key in the last 10 minutes.

Setting or changing your password signs out all your other sessions.

## Two-factor authentication

With a password set, you can require a code from an authenticator app as well:

<Steps>

1. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up**.
2. Add the account to your authenticator app with **Open in authenticator app**, or type the **Secret key** shown.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and click **Verify and enable**.

</Steps>

From then on, password sign-ins ask for a code. To turn it off, click **Disable** and enter a current code (not one you just used to sign in; wait for the next).

## Passkeys

Passkeys let you sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or a security key. Click **Add passkey**, give it a name you'll recognize (like "Work laptop"), and follow your device's prompt. Each passkey is listed with a button to remove it.

<Aside title="Confirm it's you">
Adding or removing a passkey, and setting up two-factor authentication, need a recent sign-in. If it has been a while, the app asks for your current password; without a password, sign out and back in, then try again within 15 minutes.
</Aside>

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists every browser signed in as you: the browser and system, IP address, how it signed in (Nostr key, password or passkey) and when it was last active. The one you're using is marked **this device**. End any session you don't recognize with its button, or click **Sign out other sessions** to sign out everywhere else. Their push notifications stop too.

**Media** shows every file stored on your relay's Blossom server: pictures, videos, audio and documents that people upload for their posts and profiles.

<Image src={media} alt="The Media page: stat tiles for Blobs (24), Storage (470 KB), Pending review (2, highlighted) and a By type bar, filters for status, type and owner, and a grid of image thumbnails with their sizes, two of them marked 'pending'." />

## At a glance

The tiles at the top show how many files there are (**Blobs**), how much space they take (**Storage**), how many are **Pending review**, and a bar of space by file type. Click **Pending review** to see just those.

Below, filter by status (**approved**, **pending**, **blocked**), by type (images, videos, audio, documents, text), or by owner (paste an npub or hex and press Enter). The grid loads more as you scroll.

## Look closer

Click a thumbnail to open it large. You see its status, type, size, upload time, hash (with a copy button) and who uploaded it. Use the arrows to move to the next file, and **Open** to view the original.

From there you can:

- **Approve** a pending or blocked file, so it is served again;
- **Block** it, so it stays stored but isn't served;
- **Delete** it from storage. The confirmation offers **Also block this hash from being re-uploaded** (on by default).

To act on many files, hover a tile and tick its box (or **Select all**), then use **Approve**, **Block** or **Delete** in the bar at the bottom.

## Blocked hashes

Files deleted with "also block" are remembered by their hash and refused if anyone uploads or mirrors them again. **Blocked hashes** at the top right lists them with the reason and date; **Unblock** lets that file be uploaded again.

## On your phone

Swipe a tile right to approve it, or left to delete it. Long-press a tile to start selecting.

<Image src={phone} alt="The Media page on a phone with stat tiles and a two-column grid of thumbnails." width={390} />

<Aside title="Review uploads before they go public">
Turn on **Require approval** in [Settings → Blossom](/relay/admin/settings/#blossom) and new uploads wait as *pending* until a moderator approves them here. The same section sets the upload size limit, the default quota per user, who may upload and which file types are allowed. Per-person quotas are on each [user's page](/relay/admin/users/#details).
</Aside>

<Aside>
Viewing media needs `media.view` (every role); approving, blocking and deleting need `media.moderate` (moderators and up).
</Aside>

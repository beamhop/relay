beamhop gives you your own place on Nostr. It is one small program that does the job of several:

- **A Nostr relay.** Any Nostr app can connect to it to publish and read notes.
- **A media server.** People can upload images and video to it using [Blossom](https://github.com/hzrd149/blossom).
- **An admin app.** A web app you can install on your phone to watch traffic, manage people, handle reports and change settings.
- **A plugin system.** Add your own rules, routes, jobs and admin screens.

Everything runs in one process and answers on one port. You don't need to set up a database server, a separate media service or a dashboard: the defaults work out of the box, and you can switch to PostgreSQL or S3 storage later.

<Aside type="tip" title="New to Nostr?">
  Nostr is an open network where your identity is a key pair, not an account on someone's server. Relays store and
  pass on messages. Running your own relay means your community's notes and media live somewhere you control.
</Aside>
